Under the fast–track Permanent Residency route (Regulation 6(2)), non–EU investors must purchase a brand–new residential property with a minimum value of €300,000 (+ applicable VAT) directly from a developer, using funds transferred entirely from abroad. Additionally, the main applicant must demonstrate a verified overseas annual income of at least €50,000 (plus €15,000 for a spouse and €10,000 for each dependent child). This grants permanent residency for life without requiring continuous physical presence – a single visit every two years is sufficient to maintain status.
