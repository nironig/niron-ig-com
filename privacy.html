Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information

Last updated: November 2026

Data Controller: NIRON Invest Group
Registered in Cyprus · Contact: administracja@niron-ig.com

1. Who We Are

NIRON Invest Group ("we", "us", "our") is an independent property advisory firm providing buyer representation and investment consultation services relating to Cyprus real estate. This privacy policy explains how we handle personal data collected through our UK consultation landing page at uk.niron-ig.com.

We are committed to protecting your personal information and complying with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1a. UK Representative

As a company established in Cyprus (EU) that offers services to individuals in the United Kingdom, we are in the process of formally designating a UK Representative in accordance with Article 27 UK GDPR. In the meantime, all data protection enquiries from UK residents may be directed to us directly at administracja@niron-ig.com and will be handled promptly.

2. What Personal Data We Collect

When you submit the consultation request form on our website, we collect:

  • Full name — to address you correctly and personalise the consultation
  • Direct phone number — to contact you to arrange your consultation call
  • Primary investment objective — to prepare a relevant consultation
  • Allocated capital budget range — to ensure our advisory is appropriate for your situation

We do not collect payment card data, government identification numbers, or any special category data.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Contact you within 24 hours to schedule your 30-minute consultation call
  • Prepare for and conduct the consultation relevant to your stated objectives
  • Follow up after the consultation if you have expressed interest in our services

We will not use your data for automated marketing campaigns, profiling, or sell it to third parties.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data on the basis of legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) UK GDPR) — specifically, our legitimate interest in responding to your direct enquiry for a consultation service. We have assessed that this interest does not override your rights and expectations when voluntarily submitting a consultation request.

Where you have provided explicit consent during the form submission, processing also relies on consent (Article 6(1)(a) UK GDPR).

5. International Data Transfers

NIRON Invest Group operates from Cyprus. When you submit a consultation request, your data will be transferred to and processed by our team in Cyprus. Cyprus is a member state of the European Union and subject to GDPR, which provides an equivalent level of data protection. This transfer is made under Article 45 UK GDPR (adequacy decision).

We use Make.com (Celonis SE, Germany) as a data processing tool to route form submissions securely. Make.com processes data under standard contractual clauses and is compliant with UK GDPR.

6. How Long We Keep Your Data

  • If you attend the consultation and do not proceed further — your data is retained for 12 months and then deleted.
  • If you become a client — your data is retained for the duration of our engagement plus 6 years for legal and contractual record-keeping purposes.
  • If you do not respond to our follow-up within 30 days of form submission — your data is deleted.

7. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to erasure — request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restriction — request that we limit how we process your data
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a structured, commonly used format
  • Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at administracja@niron-ig.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies and Tracking

This website may use cookies and third-party tracking technologies, including:

  • Google Tag Manager / Google Analytics — anonymous usage statistics (page views, session duration). No personally identifiable information is collected.
  • Meta Pixel — to measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns. We do not use this data to identify you personally.

You may disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. Disabling cookies will not prevent you from submitting the consultation form.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. Form submissions are transmitted over an encrypted HTTPS connection.
